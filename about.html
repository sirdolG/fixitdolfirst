ABOUT FIXITFIRST

Good repairs start with good information.

We built FixItFirst because not every household problem needs an expensive service call.

✓ Safety First

OUR PURPOSE

Helping people make better repair decisions.

When something goes wrong at home, the first question is usually "How much will this cost?"

We think the first question should be "What is actually wrong?"

FixItFirst helps you understand the likely cause, the level of difficulty, what the repair may cost, and whether it is safe to attempt yourself.

HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps.

01

Tell us what happened

Describe the sounds, symptoms, damage or changes you noticed.

02

Get a likely diagnosis

Our diagnosis system compares your symptoms with common household problems.

03

Decide what to do

Follow the suggested steps or know when it is time to call a professional.
