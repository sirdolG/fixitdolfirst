SAFETY FIRST

Stop. Stay safe. Get help when needed.

Not every household problem should be treated as a DIY repair.

01

Fire or smoke

Leave the area if there is an active fire or dangerous amount of smoke and contact your local emergency service.

02

Electrical danger

Sparks, exposed wiring, burning smells or electrical fires should not be treated as ordinary DIY repairs.

03

Major flooding

If it is safe, stop the water source. Keep away from electrical equipment that has come into contact with water.

04

Serious structural damage

Large structural cracks, collapsing ceilings or other major building damage require professional assessment.

When in doubt, don't take the risk.

FixItFirst can help with everyday maintenance, but no website can replace an emergency service or qualified professional inspection.
